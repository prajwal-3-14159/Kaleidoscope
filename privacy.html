Privacy Policy

Kaleidoscope does not collect your personal data. This page explains in plain language what that means in practice.

Last updated: 3 November 2026

The short version

  • There are no analytics, advertising, cookies, user accounts or tracking of any kind.
  • Where you zoom, what you export and your benchmark results are never sent to us or to anyone else.
  • A few settings are saved in your own browser (listed below). You can clear them whenever you like.

Who is responsible

Kaleidoscope is a web application developed and maintained as the Kaleidoscope project (“we”, “us”). The easiest way to contact us is to open an issue in the project’s GitHub repository. Issues there are public, so please do not post personal information.

How the site is delivered

Kaleidoscope is a static website. Its pages, scripts, images and fonts are plain files; the site has no server-side code and no database of its own. The files are served over HTTPS by Azure Static Web Apps, a Microsoft service.

When your browser asks for those files, Microsoft’s infrastructure may keep standard server logs, such as your IP address, your browser’s user-agent string, the time of the request and the address requested. These logs exist for security and operations. We do not run analytics and we do not use any logs to identify or track visitors. How Microsoft handles that data is described in the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

Apart from these ordinary requests for the site’s own files, nothing is sent from your browser to a server while you use Kaleidoscope.

What stays in your browser

All rendering, on the GPU or the CPU, happens on your own device. The views you explore, the images you export and your benchmark runs are never uploaded. Exported PNG images are created in your browser and saved to your device only when you download them.

The site uses your browser’s localStorage (not cookies) to remember a few things on your device:

KeyWhat it holds
kaleidoscope.langYour language choice, English or Japanese. It is also saved when you read a page in one of those languages, so the explorer and the pages open in the language you prefer.
kaleidoscope.bench.*Your benchmark history and preferences: the scores, the date of each run, the name of your graphics device (GPU) as reported by your browser, and your benchmark settings. This lets you compare runs on your own device.

This data never leaves your device and we cannot see it. Because it is used only to remember your own choices on your own device, the site does not show a cookie banner. You can delete it at any time with your browser’s “clear site data” (or “cookies and site data”) setting, or by browsing in a private window, which discards it when the window is closed.

Shared links

The address of the explorer carries the current view after a #: for example the fractal, centre point, zoom level, power and palette. This lets you bookmark a view or send it to someone. Browsers do not send the part after # to the server when they request a page, and the link contains nothing about you, so sharing it shares only those numbers. If you paste a link into a chat, a social network or an email, that service will of course see the link you pasted.

Fonts and third-party content

The typefaces (Outfit and Space Grotesk) are hosted on this site, so your browser does not contact Google Fonts or any other third party to display the pages. The site does not load third-party scripts, trackers, advertising, analytics or embedded content.

Links to other websites, such as GitHub, lead to services with their own privacy policies. We are not responsible for them, and a link is not an endorsement.

Benchmark and video recording

The benchmark runs entirely on your device and saves its results only to local storage, as described above. You can optionally record a video of a benchmark run. The recording is made by your browser (using the standard MediaRecorder API) and kept on your device; it is saved to disk only if you choose to download it, and it is never uploaded.

Children

Kaleidoscope is not directed at children under 13. Because the site collects no personal data from anyone, there is no children’s data for us to collect, keep or delete.

Your rights and questions

Because the Kaleidoscope project holds no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete. The only data connected to your visit that exists outside your device is the standard server logging by Microsoft described above; for requests about that, please see the Microsoft Privacy Statement. If you have a question about this policy, you are welcome to open an issue on GitHub.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example if Kaleidoscope gains a feature that handles data differently. The date at the top shows when it last changed, and earlier versions are available in the project’s public history on GitHub.
