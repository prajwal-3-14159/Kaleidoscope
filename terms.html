Health and safety

Photosensitivity warning. Colour cycling and the benchmark can show quickly changing, high-contrast patterns and colours. For a small number of people with photosensitive epilepsy, patterns like these can trigger seizures. If you or a family member has epilepsy or has ever had a seizure, speak to a doctor before using these features, and stop at once if you feel dizzy, disoriented or unwell.

Colour cycling is off unless you turn it on (press C), and you can cancel a benchmark run at any time (press Esc).

Deep zooms, high-resolution exports and especially the benchmark put a heavy load on your GPU and CPU, sometimes for a few minutes. Your device may become warm, the fans may spin up, the battery may drain quickly, and the page or the whole device may become slow or unresponsive. Save your work elsewhere first, close heavy applications, and avoid running the benchmark on a device that is already hot or low on battery. You use these features at your own risk.

The benchmark

The benchmark gives an indicative score for how fast Kaleidoscope runs on your device. It is not a professional or standardised measurement. Results vary with the browser and its version, graphics drivers, power mode, temperature and whatever else is running in the background, and scores may not be comparable between versions of Kaleidoscope or between different devices. Please do not rely on them for purchasing, warranty or similar decisions.

The Learn pages (the encyclopedia) are educational and deliberately simplified. We try to be accurate, but we make no guarantee that they are complete or free of errors, and they are not professional or academic advice. For anything important, please check primary sources.

Images produced by the explorer are numerical approximations. At extreme zoom levels, with non-integer powers, or on different devices and engines, they can contain rendering artefacts or differ slightly from one another.
